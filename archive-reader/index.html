Before you buy

Know exactly what it does.

  • Current systemDesigned for macOS 14 or newer.
  • First launchThis direct-download release is not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it, open System Settings, choose Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway beside Archive Reader.
  • Supported inputOfficial Instagram and Facebook exports requested in JSON format. Common message formats and Instagram media indexes are supported.
  • Archive limitsThe app can only show content Meta included. It cannot recover deleted, withheld, or missing data.
  • Format changesMeta may change export formats. Review the current compatibility notes before purchasing.
  • What the purchase includesThe current app version and its included instructions. Personalized conversion, archive repair, format adaptation, and live support are not included.
