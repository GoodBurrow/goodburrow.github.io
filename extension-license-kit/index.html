Before you buy

Make sure this is the licensing model you need.

This kit solves one specific problem: selling a permanent upgrade for a WebExtensions-based browser extension through Stripe.

This kit fits if you need:

  • A one-time purchase that permanently unlocks paid features.
  • A licensing example designed for Firefox and Chromium-based WebExtensions. The recorded clean installation test used Firefox; test the completed integration in every browser you plan to release.
  • A signed license that the extension can verify without staying online.
  • Stripe Checkout and a small fulfillment worker under your control.
  • No required customer account, database, or hosted licensing subscription.

Choose another system if you need:

  • Monthly or annual subscriptions, trials, or usage-based billing.
  • Licenses that can be remotely revoked after they are issued.
  • Customer accounts, device limits, or license syncing across devices.
  • A fully hosted licensing service that you do not deploy yourself.
  • A complete extension starter, application backend, or custom integration.
  • Licensing for software that is not based on WebExtensions.

Maintenance expectations

  • The offline verifier has no third-party runtime dependencies, and issued licenses do not expire.
  • No recurring maintenance schedule or future updates are included with the purchase.
  • Test and maintain your own integration, including reviewing relevant changes from Stripe, your hosting provider, browser extension platforms, and browser stores.
