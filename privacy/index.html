Information used to build and manage your site

Good Burrow founding pilot privacy notice.

Information collected

We collect contact, business, payment-reference, website-intake, revision, and support information needed to deliver the managed website service. Payment-card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored in this application.

Public-source concepts

Before contact, we may use limited public business information to prepare a private, unindexed concept. We do not publish it on the business's domain or represent that it has been approved.

Service providers

Information may be processed by providers used for payments, transactional email, website hosting, analytics, forms, and production publishing. We limit access to what each service requires.

Retention and choices

Prospects may decline further contact. Customers may request corrections or an export of their supplied content. After cancellation, customer content is retained for a limited handoff period described in the service terms, except where records must be kept for tax, fraud, or legal purposes.

Contact

Business contact details will be published before live service begins.
