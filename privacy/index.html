Privacy

Last updated August 11, 2026

This website

Good Burrow does not use advertising trackers or create visitor accounts on this website. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitor IP addresses for security under GitHub's privacy statement.

Burrowmarks

Burrowmarks stores encrypted bookmark data inside your Firefox profile. Good Burrow does not receive your saved links, notes, master password, or recovery key.

Social Keepsake

Social Keepsake reads selected social-media export files on your Mac. Good Burrow does not receive the messages, media, account names, or other contents of an archive you open in the app.

Purchases and messages

Payments are handled by the payment provider shown at checkout. Good Burrow does not receive your full card number. If you contact us or make a purchase, we keep the information reasonably needed to respond, provide the product, and maintain required business records.

Contact

Privacy notices can be submitted through the Good Burrow public repository. Do not include private archive contents, passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.
