Managed website service terms

Good Burrow founding pilot service terms.

Service

The founding service includes the finished informational website shown in the customer's private preview, managed hosting, SSL, a protected contact form, and constrained maintenance controls for approved content and prepared-section visibility. The previewed design is offered as shown; the service includes factual verification and correction, not a redesign or revision round. Ecommerce, scheduling, memberships, custom integrations, and additional production work are outside this offer.

Billing and cancellation

The launch charge is paid once at checkout and includes the first 30 days of managed service. Monthly service begins after that included period and renews until canceled. Customers may cancel before the next renewal through the billing portal or by written request. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period.

Refunds and disputes

Duplicate or erroneous charges are refunded. If we decline the project or fail to provide the agreed review version, the launch charge is refunded. After the customer submits intake and production work begins, any refund of the launch charge is based on the portion of the agreed work not delivered. Monthly service charges are not prorated after a billing period begins unless required by law. Contact us promptly about a billing or service problem so it can be reviewed before a payment dispute is filed.

Ownership

The customer retains its domain, trademarks, original copy, and supplied media. The managed website service retains its design system and hosted implementation unless the customer purchases the offered project transfer.

Customer approval

The customer confirms it has authority to use supplied names, claims, logos, and media. Good Burrow corrects inaccurate business facts before launch. Nothing is placed on the customer's domain until written approval.

No performance guarantee

The service does not guarantee search rankings, leads, revenue, or uninterrupted third-party services.

Contact

Business contact details will be published before live service begins.
